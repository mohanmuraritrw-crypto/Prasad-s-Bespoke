Privacy note

This site uses cookies and analytics to understand how visitors use it, so we can make it easier to find the right outfit and get in touch.

What we use

  • Google Ads (Google tag) measures whether visitors who come from our ads go on to contact us, for example by tapping a WhatsApp button.
  • Microsoft Clarity records anonymous usage, such as taps, scrolling and heatmaps, so we can see which parts of the site are confusing or unused.

These services may set cookies and collect device and browser details, pages visited and interactions. We do not sell this information. You can read how they handle data in the Google Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

WhatsApp

When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message ready for you to send. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Any details you share with us there are used only to reply to you and arrange your consultation.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or browse in a private window. The site will still work.
