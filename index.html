Mission

Cut by hand.Fitted to you.Worn for a lifetime.

Suits, wedding gowns and Ethnic Wear made entirely to your measure, from the first sketch to the final press.

Meet our atelier

From Fabric
to heirloom

Seven steps · Three fittings
Step I

The first sitting

Conversation, cloth and measurements. We learn how you stand and move.

Step II

The pattern

A paper pattern drafted by hand for you alone, kept on file for life.

Step III

The cut

Your cloth is chalked and cut by hand at the cutting table.

Step IV

Baste fitting

Loosely tacked together so we can shape it on you.

Step V

Forward fitting

Canvas, lapels and sleeves refined closer to your posture.

Step VI

Hand finishing

Silk-twist buttonholes, pick stitching and your chosen lining.

Step VII

The final press

Pressed, delivered and cared for as long as you wear it.

Meet our ateliers

Bridal Couture

Gowns, lehengas and designer bridal wear, embroidered in our atelier and fitted in the weeks before the wedding.

Hand Embroidery

Zardozi, aari and resham work, placed stitch by stitch to your design.

Inner Corsetry

A structured bodice built inside the gown for support through the whole day.

Lace Appliqué

Lace motifs cut and placed by hand so the seams disappear.

The Train

Chapel or cathedral length, weighted and bustled for the reception.

Lehenga Can-can

Layered netting gives the skirt its volume without the weight.

The Final Fitting

A last fitting days before the wedding, with your shoes and jewellery.

The craft

The details we never skip

01

Full Canvas

A floating horsehair canvas shapes the chest and moulds to you over years of wear.

02

Hand-padded Lapel

Hundreds of small stitches give the lapel its soft, natural roll.

03

Working Cuffs

Surgeon's cuffs with buttonholes that open, finished by hand.

04

Pick Stitching

A fine hand stitch along the edges, visible only up close.

05

Hand Buttonholes

Silk twist sewn by hand around a corded edge, one buttonhole at a time.

06

Your Lining

Bemberg or silk linings chosen with you, with your monogram inside.
